# FIRE-X — Firefighter Rescue & Reconnaissance Robot

**Developed by Team DEEPNEX | Smart India Hackathon 2026**

##  Core Value Proposition
> **"KEEP PEOPLE OUT OF THE FIRST LOOK"**
> 
> The FIRE-X robot scouts hazardous zones first, collects real-time hazard data, sends actionable information, and supports safer human deployment before rescue crews enter dangerous environments.


##  Problem Statement & Market Need
* **5,971** fire-accident cases reported in India (NCRB ADSI 2024).
* **5,888** deaths reported due to fire incidents.
* **60.4%** of fire deaths occurred in residential environments.

##  Feature & System Comparison

| Capability | Fire Rover (Site System) | Indian Fire Robot (XENA/KRUSHNA) | **Our FIRE-X Concept** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Role** | Early detection + suppression | Remote fire suppression | **Reconnaissance + safety intelligence** |
| **Mobility** | Site-installed / fixed deployment | Tracked / mobile | **Tracked, compact** |
| **Sensing** | Thermal + smoke + flame | Thermal + video | **mmWave + thermal / environmental** |
| **Communication** | Networked system | Remote radio / control | **Sub-GHz LoRa mesh + Wi-Fi video** |
| **Key Distinction** | Protected-site suppression | High-capacity water / foam monitor | **Hazard data + rescue reconnaissance** |

##  Key Technical Differentiators

* **LoRa Mesh:** Sub-GHz relay nodes maintain continuous telemetry through heavily obstructed or signal-denied areas.
* **mmWave Radar:** Enables human movement and range sensing independent of smoke density or visible light.
* **Multi-Sensor Intelligence:** Combines thermal, gas, distance, and IMU data for total situational and hazard awareness.
* **Rescue-First Approach:** Maps structural conditions and hazardous hot spots prior to human firefighter entry.

##  Target Industries & Deployment Sites

* Fire & rescue departments
* High-rise residential apartments
* Hotels, shopping malls & commercial buildings
* Warehouses & logistics hubs
* Chemical & petrochemical plants
* Tunnels, basements & confined spaces
