# FIRE-X — Firefighter Rescue & Reconnaissance Robot[cite: 27]

**Developed by Team DEEPNEX | Smart India Hackathon 2026**[cite: 27]

##  Core Value Proposition
> **"KEEP PEOPLE OUT OF THE FIRST LOOK"**[cite: 27]
> 
> The FIRE-X robot scouts hazardous zones first, collects real-time hazard data, sends actionable information, and supports safer human deployment before rescue crews enter dangerous environments[cite: 27].


##  Problem Statement & Market Need
* **5,971** fire-accident cases reported in India (NCRB ADSI 2024)[cite: 27].
* **5,888** deaths reported due to fire incidents[cite: 27].
* **60.4%** of fire deaths occurred in residential environments[cite: 27].

##  Key Technical Differentiators

* **LoRa Mesh:** Sub-GHz relay nodes maintain continuous telemetry through heavily obstructed or signal-denied areas[cite: 27].
* **mmWave Radar:** Enables human movement and range sensing independent of smoke density or visible light[cite: 27].
* **Multi-Sensor Intelligence:** Combines thermal, gas, distance, and IMU data for total situational and hazard awareness[cite: 27].
* **Rescue-First Approach:** Maps structural conditions and hazardous hot spots prior to human firefighter entry[cite: 27].
